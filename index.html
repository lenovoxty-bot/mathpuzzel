Privacy Policy

Math Puzzle

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 • Version 3.0

1. Introduction

Welcome to Math Puzzle ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, transparent mobile gaming experience.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Zero Account Registration Required:

Math Puzzle does not require user accounts, emails, or logins. We do not collect or store personal identifiers, phone numbers, or passwords on remote servers.

3. Local Device Data

All gameplay state is stored exclusively on your device (Android SharedPreferences):

  • Cleared levels (1 to 500) and unlocked stages.
  • Stars collected, completion times, and speed rush records.
  • Brain IQ progression score.
  • Haptic vibration and audio sound preferences.
  • Hint tokens inventory.

You can clear all local data at any time via Settings > Reset Progress.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Math Puzzle displays ads via Google AdMob (banner ads, level completion interstitial ads, and optional rewarded video ads for hints). AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP address, and diagnostic performance metrics.

For more details, visit Google Ad Policies.

5. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed by Google AdMob to load advertisements.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely for optional tactile haptic feedback.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Math Puzzle does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. All gameplay state remains solely on the device.
