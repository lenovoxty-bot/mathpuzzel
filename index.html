Legal & Privacy Policy

Math Puzzle

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 • Version 3.0

1. Introduction

Welcome to Math Puzzle ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, transparent mobile gaming experience. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how your privacy is respected when you download and play Math Puzzle.

By installing and using Math Puzzle, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Zero Account Registration Required:

Math Puzzle does not require user accounts, emails, social logins, or passwords to play. We do not collect or store your name, phone number, address, financial records, or contacts on remote servers.

3. Information Collected and Stored Locally

To deliver an uninterrupted puzzle experience, the App stores gameplay progress strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Level Progress & Unlocks: Cleared levels (1 to 120) and unlocked stages.
  • Scores & Stars: Stars collected per puzzle, completion times, and speed records.
  • Brain IQ Score: Calculated IQ progression rating based on solved levels.
  • Game Settings: Haptic vibration preferences and audio sound effects toggles.
  • Hint Tokens: Remaining hint tokens inventory.

Note: You can reset all local gameplay data at any time directly inside the App by visiting Settings > Reset Progress.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

Math Puzzle uses industry-standard third-party SDKs to facilitate app stability, display advertisements, and reward player engagement. These third-party services may collect automated telemetry and identifiers as governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Serves banner ads, interstitial level-transition ads, and optional rewarded video ads for in-game hint rewards. AdMob may collect device advertising IDs (AAID), IP address, coarse location, device technical specifications, and ad performance metrics.
    Privacy Policy: Google Ad Policies • How AdMob uses data
  • Google Play Services: Provides core Android runtime libraries and diagnostic capabilities.
    Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

5. Your Choices & Opting Out of Targeted Ads

You have complete control over personalized advertising on your Android device:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: On your Android device, navigate to Settings > Google > Ads and choose "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
  • Opt Out of Interest-Based Advertising: You can turn off personalized ads through Google Ad Settings at adssettings.google.com.

6. Device Permissions

Math Puzzle requests only the minimal permissions required to function properly:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Google AdMob to load advertisements and check network reachability.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide optional tactile haptic feedback on button presses and puzzle solves (can be disabled in Settings).

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Math Puzzle is a general audience arithmetic puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. All gameplay state remains solely on the device. Advertisements are configured to adhere to Google Play Families Policy guidelines.

8. Data Security

Because we do not operate external user account databases, your sensitive personal information is not stored or transmitted to external company servers. All network traffic generated by Google AdMob SDK operates over encrypted SSL/TLS (HTTPS) channels.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update our Privacy Policy to reflect app updates, new features, or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated effective date.
